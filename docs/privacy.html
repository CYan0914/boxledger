BoxLedger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

BoxLedger is an inventory and bookkeeping app for second-hand resellers. It is built to work without an account, and the records you enter stay on your device.

What the app stores

  • Item details you enter: title, cost, source, bin code, list price, platform, notes, and whether the item is in stock, listed or sold.
  • Sale details you enter: sale price, shipping you paid, fee snapshot, and tax reserve percentage.
  • Photos you take or pick for an item.
  • Your settings: stale reminder days, default tax reserve, and edited platform fee rates.

All of this is written to the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not operate a server for this app and we cannot read your records.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data, contacts, or health data.
  • No marketplace credentials. BoxLedger does not connect to Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Etsy, Depop or Vinted.

Purchases

BoxLedger Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and handles refunds; we never see your name, card number or billing address. The app asks the App Store on your device whether a Pro purchase is active, and stores that yes/no answer locally.

Photos

Photos are copied into the app's own storage when you attach them to an item. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting an item removes its record; deleting all data removes every record and setting.

Export

The CSV export is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is entirely your choice.

Deleting your data

Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored, because there is no copy elsewhere.

Children

BoxLedger is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off the device, this page will say so before that version ships, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
