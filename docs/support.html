Support

Inventory and real-profit tracking for second-hand resellers.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought BoxLedger Pro. If nothing is found, check that the purchase appears in your Apple ID's purchase history.

The app says the item cap is reached

The free tier records up to 30 items. Recording a sale on an item you already have never stops working. Pro lifts the cap and unlocks the CSV export.

Fees look wrong for a platform

Every rate in the app comes from the editable fee table in Settings. The defaults are a snapshot of each platform's published rates, dated in the app. If a platform has changed its rate, edit that row and save; profit is recalculated from the table.

Where is my data?

On your device. There is no account and no cloud copy. Settings → Delete all data removes everything permanently. See the Privacy Policy.

Exporting records for taxes

Settings → Export CSV produces a file with one row per sold item: date, item, cost, sale price, platform fee and profit. It opens in Numbers or Excel.
